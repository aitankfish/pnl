## SLIDE 5 — The Journey (river → tank) `[32s]`

*Slow down. This is the story moment. Let the animation play.*

> "Meet a fish. She has an idea in a quiet river.
> No audience. No money. Nobody to tell her if it's any good."

*Fish swims to the waterfall. Pause.*

> "So she takes a leap. And she lands in **the FishTank**."

*The tank fills in. Pause as believers and doubters swim in.*

> "Inside the tank, two kinds of fish.
> **Believers** — people who think the idea will work — back it with real money.
> **Doubters** — people who don't — bet against it."

*Watch the conviction meter fill.*

> "Both sides put conviction on the line. And when the clock runs out..."

*The APPROVED badge pops.*

> "...the crowd has spoken."

*Advance.*

# 🎬 Delivery Notes

## Overall Tone Arc
- **Slides 1-3** — **Urgent, serious.** The world is changing. Problem mode.
- **Slide 4** — **Softer. Hopeful.** Here's who we are.
- **Slide 5** — **Storyteller.** Slow down. Let the metaphor breathe.
- **Slide 6** — **Confident expert.** This is LIVE. Let it prove itself.
- **Slides 7-8** — **Building momentum.** The mechanics click.
- **Slides 9-11** — **Business closer.** Numbers land hard.
- **Slide 12** — **Quiet conviction.** Let the room hold its breath.

## Key Phrases to Rehearse
1. *"When people lose the work they deserved, they build."*
2. *"Not gatekept like VCs. Not lawless like speculation. Merit wins."*
3. *"The market is alive."*
4. *"The fish is still in the tank, but her idea is flourishing like a tree."*
5. *"Build and earn. No dilution. No pitch meetings."*
6. *"Seven hundred and eighty-eight million dollars a year."*
7. *"We are here to make every idea a reality."*

You have about **40 seconds of buffer** in a 4-minute slot. Use it for pauses, not words.

## Things NOT To Do
- ❌ Don't apologize or hedge ("We think...", "We hope...")
- ❌ Don't say "sort of", "kind of", "basically", "just"
- ❌ Don't rush the demo — it's your strongest asset
- ❌ Don't read from the slides — judges can read
- ❌ Don't end with a question you haven't set up
- ❌ Don't fill silence after "Thank you."

## Things TO Do

## The Ultimate Test
When you finish practicing, ask yourself: **"Could the judges tell the fish story back to me in one sentence?"**

If yes, you've won them over. If no, keep simplifying the story in slide 5.
