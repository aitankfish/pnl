# PNL — THE WILDEST PITCH DECK NARRATIVE
## "From a Bedroom in Houston to a Global Launchpad"

---

# THE PITCH STRUCTURE (12 Minutes)

This is designed as a **story-first pitch** — not a slide deck you read, but a journey you take the audience on. Every section has a **spoken narrative** (what you say) and **visual beats** (what's on screen).

---

## ACT I: THE FIRE (3 min)
*Goal: Make the audience feel the pain. Make it personal. Make it universal.*

---

### SLIDE 1: THE HOOK (30 sec)
**Screen:** Black. Single sentence fades in.

> *"Every person in this room has a dream they never started."*

**You say:**

"Everyone has dreams. As kids, we all believe we can change the world. But as we grow up, life gets in the way. Visa issues. Bank accounts. Geography. The dreams don't die because they're bad — they die because the system never gave them a chance.

I know this because I lived it."

---

### SLIDE 2: MY JOURNEY — THE SEARCH (90 sec)
**Screen:** Timeline visual — each venture appearing and fading, building a trail.

**You say:**

"I've been in crypto since 2020. I'm an engineer. And for the last two years, I've been searching for the thing I was meant to build.

First, I built **The Sol Store** — a merch platform where crypto communities could buy gear using Solana or their community tokens. I learned about communities.

Then I launched **Dinero** — a meme coin where I tried to build a community around money itself. I learned about tokenomics and hype cycles.

Then I went into **NFT collectibles** — tried to build something meaningful, but the tools weren't there yet. I learned about digital ownership.

Then I got obsessed with something bigger. I researched how to use the **hydroelectric power of Nepal** to build a Bitcoin mining operation. I knew the math worked. The Himalayan rivers produce some of the cheapest electricity on Earth. I just needed funding.

*(pause)*

And that's where everything broke."

---

### SLIDE 3: THE WALL (60 sec)
**Screen:** Big bold text: **"I couldn't find $50,000."**

**You say:**

"I'm in the United States. I have engineering skills. I have a network. I have a viable business plan. And I could not find anyone to fund a Bitcoin mining operation with hydropower.

I reached out to everyone I knew. No luck.

And then the question hit me like a truck:

*If I — an engineer in America — can't get funded... how does a developer in Pokhara, Nepal? How does a builder in Lagos? In Jakarta? In Medellín?*

The answer was simple: **they don't.**

Less than 1% of startups get VC funding. And that 1% is overwhelmingly in San Francisco, New York, and London. The system isn't broken for some people. It's broken for most of the world."

---

## ACT II: THE DISCOVERY (3 min)
*Goal: Show how the idea evolved through real experience, not theory.*

---

### SLIDE 4: FISHTANK — THE FIRST ATTEMPT (60 sec)
**Screen:** FishTank logo/concept → simple fundraising platform visual.

**You say:**

"In December 2024, I said to myself: *'It's time to build. I'm an engineer.'*

I started building **FishTank** — a blockchain-based fundraising platform. Think GoFundMe, but global, on Solana, for project founders anywhere in the world. No banks required. No VCs needed.

Five months of building. A working prototype.

But there was a fundamental problem I couldn't solve: **trust.**

Why would a stranger on the internet send money to another stranger's idea? In crypto, scammers are everywhere. 98.6% of tokens launched on Pump.fun were rug pulls. How do you build trust in a trustless world?"

---

### SLIDE 5: THE COLLISION (90 sec)
**Screen:** Three forces converging into one point — animated visual.
- Tokenization
- Prediction Markets
- Meme Token Mania

**You say:**

"In May 2025, I attended the **Solana Accelerator Event** in New York. Everyone was talking about tokenization — how everything in finance would eventually become a token.

I came back to Houston. And for three months, I just... thought.

I kept replaying three things in my mind:

**One:** I'm an entrepreneur who can't raise funds. There are millions like me.

**Two:** I watched the meme token craze of January 2024. Seven million tokens launched. $500 million lost to scams. Degens were desperate for *something real* to trade.

**Three:** I watched prediction markets nail the 2024 election when every poll got it wrong. Polymarket processed $3.3 billion in wagers. When real money is on the line, people do real research.

And then it clicked.

*(pause for effect)*

What if the way to solve trust in fundraising... was to make funding itself a prediction market? What if instead of asking people to *donate* to ideas, you let them *bet* on which ideas deserve to exist?"

---

### SLIDE 6: THE BREAKTHROUGH

Not from a whiteboard at Stanford. Not from a VC brainstorm in Sand Hill Road.

From a guy in Houston who couldn't raise $50K, who spent two years building things that almost worked, and who finally saw the one idea that connects everything:

## ACT III: THE MACHINE (3 min)
*Goal: Show that this isn't just a vision — it's a working, elegant system.*

---

### SLIDE 7: HOW IT WORKS — SIMPLE VERSION (60 sec)
**Screen:** 4-step animated flow.

**You say:**

"Here's how PNL works in four steps:

**Step 1: CREATE.** A founder anywhere in the world posts their idea as a prediction market. Cost: 0.015 SOL — about two bucks. That's it.

**Step 2: VALIDATE.** The global community votes YES or NO with real money. Not likes. Not follows. Real SOL on the line. This is where the magic happens — when your wallet is involved, you do your homework.

**Step 3: RESOLVE.** The market expires. If YES wins, the idea proved itself. If NO wins, the critics were right.

**Step 4: LAUNCH.** If YES wins, the token launches *automatically* on Pump.fun with the pooled funds as liquidity. The early believers get 65% of the tokens. The founder gets 33%, vested over 12 months. No insiders. No pre-sale. Same price for everyone.

If NO wins? The critics split 95% of the pool proportionally. **You get paid to filter out bad ideas.**"

# SPEAKER NOTES & TIPS

## Emotional Beats to Hit
1. **Vulnerability** (Act I) — Let them see you struggled. Audiences invest in founders who are honest about failure.
2. **The "aha" moment** (Act II) — The collision of tokenization + prediction markets + personal pain. This is the *insight* investors remember.
3. **Simplicity** (Act III) — The four-step flow should feel inevitable. "Of course this is how it should work."
4. **Universality** (Act IV) — The Pokhara slide makes it about *everyone*, not just you.
5. **Confidence** (Close) — End like you've already won. Not arrogant — certain.

## Delivery Tips
- **Don't rush Act I.** The personal story is your unfair advantage. No one else in the room has this journey.
- **Pause after the $50K line.** Let it sit. The silence is louder than any slide.
- **On the "it clicked" moment** — physically step forward. Change your body language. This is the turning point.
- **For "Ship your dreams from your bedroom"** — deliver it looking directly at the judges, not the screen.
- **Time the "Predict and Launch" close** with the PNL brand hitting the screen.

| Likely Question | Answer |

| "How do you prevent manipulation?" | AMM constant-product formula (x*y=k). Moving price 25% requires ~10 SOL. One wallet per position. All on-chain, verifiable. |

| "What about MetaDAO?" | MetaDAO targets top-tier, curated projects with capital requirements. PNL is permissionless and global — the *long tail* of ideas. We're GoFundMe; they're Y Combinator. Different markets entirely. |

| "How do you acquire users?" | Crypto Twitter, Solana ecosystem events, founder communities. Every successful launch creates organic virality — the founder's community discovers PNL through their token. |
