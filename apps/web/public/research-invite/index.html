What are conviction markets?

Instead of pitching gatekeepers, projects earn funding from people willing to back them. Backers stake on what they believe will succeed; sustained conviction — not a one-time vote — directs resources to the projects with real traction. When a project proves out, its value is tokenized and shared between the builders and the research it stands on. It's a way to fund science and the things built on it by belief and follow-through, in the open.

Questions

Do I give up rights to my research?

No. Your work stays attributed to you. You're adding it as a foundation others can build on, and you participate in the upside of what they create — you're not signing it away.

What does it cost to add my research?

Nothing — posting your paper is free and open. Longer term we're building toward foundational researchers sharing in the upside of what's built on their work; that's a direction we're working toward, not a guaranteed return.

Is this live today?

The core is live: you can post your paper today as a public, citable foundation, others can cite and build projects on it, and conviction markets surface traction. The piece we're still building is routing upside back to foundational researchers — so it's early, and the best time to get in is while it's being shaped.
