PNL turns idea-stage fundraising into a **conviction market**. Two camps form around every idea — believers stake SOL on YES, critics stake SOL on NO. When the market closes, the winning side takes the pool. If YES wins, the idea graduates: a token launches on pump.fun with pooled liquidity, the founder earns ongoing royalties, and believers receive token airdrops. If NO wins, critics split the pool — paid for filtering noise.

This page is the 60-second tour. For the math, see [Economics](/docs/mechanics/economics). For the on-chain instructions, see [On-chain program](/docs/build/on-chain-program).

## The four-step lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plant">
    Anyone posts an idea for **0.015 SOL** (~$2 at current SOL price). Title, thesis, target raise, duration. The market opens immediately and accepts SOL stakes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stake">
    Believers stake YES. Critics stake NO. Minimum stake **0.01 SOL**. A 1.5% trade fee accrues to the platform treasury on every vote. One position per wallet per market.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve">
    At expiry, the side with more pooled SOL wins. A 5% completion fee is taken from the pool. The market enters its terminal state.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Distribute">
    **YES wins** → a token launches on pump.fun with pooled liquidity. 65% of token supply airdrops to YES voters proportional to their stake. 33% vests to the founder team. 2% to the platform.

    **NO wins** → 95% of the pool distributes to NO voters proportional to their stake.

    **Tie or threshold-not-met** → full refund. No fees taken.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Market states (in PNL's own vocabulary)

- **Living** — accepting votes, time remaining
- **Bloomed** — YES won, token launched on pump.fun
- **Withered** — NO won, critics paid out
- **Closed** — window expired without meeting threshold
- **Returned** — ineligible, stakes refunded

## What makes PNL different

| | Memecoin launchpad | Standard prediction market | PNL |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What you bet on** | Existing tokens | Real-world events (elections, sports) | New ideas — does this deserve to launch? |
| **What "winning" means** | Token goes up | You called the outcome | Your conviction shaped what got built |
| **Filter on noise** | None | None | NO voters paid to filter |
| **Founder upside** | Trading fees on a token nobody wanted | n/a | Royalties only on community-validated ideas |
| **Skin in the game** | Just buyers | One-sided bet | Both believers AND critics stake real SOL |
