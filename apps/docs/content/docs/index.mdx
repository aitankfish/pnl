## The thesis in 60 seconds

Most ideas die in private notebooks. The old path to funding — venture capital, geographic proximity, who you know — funds less than 1% of decks and reaches almost none of the world's builders. Meanwhile token launchpads dump 7M+ memecoins into the market with no filter at all. 98.6% are scams.

P&L closes both gaps with one mechanism:

1. **Anyone posts an idea** for ~$2.
2. A **conviction market** opens. Believers stake SOL on YES. Critics stake on NO. Both sides have skin in the game.
3. When conviction wins, the idea graduates: a token launches with pooled liquidity, the founder earns ongoing royalties, and believers receive token airdrops.
4. When skepticism wins, critics split the pool — paid for filtering noise.
5. Every idea, funded or not, joins a **permanent on-chain library**.

The crowd becomes the committee. Conviction becomes capital.
