The $PNL token is live on Solana mainnet. Contract address:

```
6QuNZJzUF7oZj3GsG7fVBfidX1cE81sXhb9Czi12pump
```

You need a Solana wallet to hold $PNL. We recommend **Phantom** — fastest, most user-friendly on mobile and desktop. Below are three paths depending on where you're starting from.

<Tip>
Every path ends with PNL in your Phantom wallet. Once you have it, you can vote on conviction markets, claim airdrops from bloomed markets, and trade through Jupiter aggregator.
</Tip>

## Step 0 — Get Phantom Wallet

Phantom is the wallet that holds your SOL and PNL. You'll need it regardless of which buying path you choose.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Phantom">
    Get Phantom from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). For desktop, install the browser extension from [phantom.app](https://phantom.app).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your wallet">
    Open Phantom and sign up with your email. Your wallet is created automatically. **Save your recovery phrase somewhere safe** — without it you cannot recover your funds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your Solana receiving address">
    Tap **Receive** and select Solana. Copy this address — you'll use it to receive SOL or PNL from exchanges in the paths below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

[Get Phantom →](https://phantom.app)

## Path A — Buy directly in Phantom (fastest)

No exchange required. Buy SOL with a card right inside Phantom, then swap to PNL.

<Steps>
  <Step title='Tap "Buy" in Phantom'>
    Open Phantom and tap the **Buy** button on the home screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue with email">
    Phantom's buy flow is powered by MoonPay or similar. Sign in with email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete KYC verification">
    First-time only. Upload an ID document. Usually approved within minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter payment & buy SOL">
    Use a debit card, credit card, or bank transfer. Buy enough SOL to cover the PNL amount you want plus a small buffer for transaction fees (~0.01 SOL).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the Swap button">
    On Phantom's home screen, tap **Swap**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap SOL → PNL">
    Set **From** to SOL and **To** to PNL. If PNL isn't in the dropdown, paste the contract address `6QuNZJzUF7oZj3GsG7fVBfidX1cE81sXhb9Czi12pump`. Confirm the swap. Done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Path B — Through Robinhood

Useful if you already have funds on Robinhood and don't want to verify with a new exchange.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deposit money on Robinhood">
    Add cash to your Robinhood account via your linked bank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy Solana (SOL)">
    Search "Solana" or "SOL" and buy the amount you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Transfer → Send">
    In Robinhood Crypto, choose **Transfer** and then **Send**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="(First time only) Verify your identity">
    Robinhood requires extra verification for crypto withdrawals. Follow the prompts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste Phantom address & send SOL">
    Paste the Solana receiving address you copied from Phantom (Step 0). Send your SOL. Usually arrives in 1-2 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap SOL → PNL in Phantom">
    Once SOL arrives in Phantom, follow Step 5 and 6 of Path A to swap to PNL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Path C — Through Coinbase

Coinbase supports PNL directly by contract address.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Coinbase & search by contract address">
    In the Coinbase app, search for `6QuNZJzUF7oZj3GsG7fVBfidX1cE81sXhb9Czi12pump`. PNL should appear as a supported asset on Solana.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy PNL">
    Select PNL, choose the amount, complete the purchase with your payment method on file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="(Optional) Send to Phantom to hold">
    If you want to use PNL on pnl.market or hold it long-term, withdraw from Coinbase to your Phantom Solana address. If you only want to trade, you can leave it on Coinbase.
  </Step>
</Steps>
